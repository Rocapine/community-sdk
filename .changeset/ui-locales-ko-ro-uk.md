Three more built-in catalogs: Korean (`ko`, 해요체), Romanian (`ro`, "tu") and Ukrainian (`uk`, "ти"), with their CLDR plural rules (`ro` one/few/other, `uk` one/few/other with 21 → one). Every catalog gains `language.ko` / `language.ro` / `language.uk` for the "Translated from …" caption.
