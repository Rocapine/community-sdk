Ten more built-in locales: Dutch (`nl`), Russian (`ru`), Greek (`el`), Hungarian (`hu`), Swedish (`sv`), Norwegian Bokmål (`nb`, also used for `no`), Danish (`da`), Croatian (`hr`), Slovak (`sk`) and Czech (`cs`), 23 in all. Russian and Croatian plurals use one/few/other like Ukrainian, Czech and Slovak one (1) / few (2–4) / other. Every locale also names these ten languages in "Translated from …", including a post the translator reports as `no`.
